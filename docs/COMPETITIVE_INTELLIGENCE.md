## 2026-09-29 — pricing/positioning
FlightClaimly public model:
- 20% standard success fee incl. VAT
- 30% total incl. VAT if legal escalation is required
- EUR 600 -> EUR 480 standard customer net
- EUR 600 -> EUR 420 legal customer net
- no upfront fee / no standard service fee without recovery

Live localized comparison cluster: ReFly, AirHelp, AirAdvisor, SkyRefund.

Guardrail: never publish unsupported broad superlatives such as "Europe's cheapest".

## AirHelp / ClaimCompass
ClaimCompass was acquired by AirHelp in 2024 and is no longer an independent current provider for new claims. Treat older comparisons accordingly.

AirHelp has broad owned authority, affiliate distribution and social proof. FlightClaimly should compete through differentiated economics, transparency, precise authority content, external corroboration, useful data and genuine customer proof rather than raw content volume.
