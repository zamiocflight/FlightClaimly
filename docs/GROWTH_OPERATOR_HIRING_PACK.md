## Job title

**Content & Growth Operator — Travel Startup (Part-Time, Long-Term)**

## Job post — ready to publish

### About FlightClaimly

FlightClaimly is a European flight-compensation service that helps passengers pursue compensation when flights are delayed, cancelled or otherwise disrupted.

We are building a modern, lean travel-tech brand and are now looking for a sharp, reliable Content & Growth Operator who can help us turn good ideas into consistent execution.

### The role

This is a hands-on part-time role for someone who enjoys social media, short-form content, research and growth.

You will help us:
- turn flight disruptions, passenger-rights topics and travel news into useful social content
- create short-form concepts for TikTok, Instagram Reels and similar channels
- write concise posts and captions
- create/edit simple visual and video assets in Canva and/or CapCut
- research airlines, airports, creators, travel communities and relevant conversations
- help distribute content instead of simply publishing and waiting
- identify creator, affiliate and partnership opportunities
- support basic Meta/social campaign execution and reporting
- track what content and channels actually generate traffic and customer actions

You will work directly with the founder. You will receive clear facts, positioning and legal guardrails; your job is to turn them into strong content and distribution.

### What we are looking for

You should have:
- excellent written English
- strong social-media instincts
- experience with Canva and/or CapCut
- ability to create concise hooks, posts and short-form concepts
- good online research skills
- basic understanding of Meta/social analytics
- attention to detail
- ability to work independently and communicate clearly
- curiosity about travel, airlines, consumer services or growth marketing

Nice to have:
- TikTok/Reels editing experience
- creator/influencer outreach
- affiliate outreach
- community management
- paid social experience
- experience working with a startup/founder

### Important

This is not a role where you generate large volumes of generic AI articles.

Flight compensation is a regulated/legal-rights topic. You must never invent eligibility conclusions, legal facts, statistics or competitor claims. We will provide verified source material and review sensitive claims.

We value accuracy, initiative and distribution thinking more than content volume.

### Hours and compensation

Initial commitment: approximately **10 hours/week**.

Target compensation range: **USD 8–12/hour**, depending on experience and the quality of your practical work.

There is potential for more responsibility and hours if the collaboration works well and FlightClaimly grows.

## First screening questions

Use these only for applicants who pass the basic filter.

1. Show one short-form post/video you personally created. What part did you do yourself and what happened after it was published?
2. If a major Lufthansa flight was cancelled today, how would you decide whether it is worth creating content around?
3. What would you check before publishing a claim such as “passengers can receive €600”?
4. If a Reel gets views but no website traffic, what would you test next?
5. Which tasks in this role are you strongest at, and which would you need help with?

## Paid practical test

Invite only the top **3 candidates**.

Suggested payment: **USD 20–30 per candidate**. The purpose is to test thinking and execution, not extract free production work.

### Candidate brief

Scenario:

> A Lufthansa flight has suffered a major disruption. FlightClaimly helps eligible passengers pursue EU261 compensation. FlightClaimly's standard success fee is 20% including VAT, so a passenger keeps 80% of compensation recovered under the standard fee. No compensation is guaranteed; eligibility depends on the flight and circumstances.

Create:

1. **One TikTok/Reel concept**
   - opening hook
   - 20–30 second structure/script
   - on-screen text ideas
   - CTA

2. **One X/social post**
   - concise and publishable
   - no invented facts

3. **One simple Canva/CapCut asset or mockup**
   - visual execution matters more than polish

4. **Distribution note — maximum 150 words**
   - where would you distribute this?
   - who is the target audience?
   - what would you measure?
   - what would you do if the first post gets little traction?

5. **Accuracy check**
   - list the factual/legal claims in your own content that you would verify before publishing.

Time expectation: approximately 60–90 minutes.

Important: candidates are not expected to independently decide whether passengers on the example flight legally qualify. We are testing whether they know where their responsibility stops.

## Interview — 20 minutes

Structure:
- 5 min: candidate explains test choices
- 5 min: challenge one decision (“why this hook/channel/CTA?”)
- 5 min: live mini-scenario — turn one factual disruption into two content angles
- 5 min: availability, communication, rate, start date and questions

We are testing:
- whether the candidate actually created the submitted work
- whether they can think without a template
- whether they accept factual/legal guardrails
- whether communication is easy and fast

## Initial 2-week trial

Do not give a new operator broad publishing autonomy on day one.

Week 1:
- learn Evidence Pack and brand guardrails
- create content backlog
- research live opportunities
- produce drafts/assets
- founder approval before publication

Week 2:
- begin scheduled execution
- basic distribution/outreach
- report outputs and results
- identify what should be repeated/stopped

Trial KPIs are execution/learning metrics, not vanity targets:
- agreed deliverables completed on time
- factual accuracy
- useful content concepts produced
- distribution actions completed
- reporting clarity
- speed of iteration after feedback

Do not judge a new hire solely on follower growth or viral views over two weeks.

## Operating guardrails

The operator MAY:
- create drafts from verified source material
- edit video/graphics
- research opportunities
- prepare creator/affiliate outreach lists
- distribute approved content
- report performance
- suggest experiments

The operator MAY NOT independently:
- decide EU261/UK261 legal eligibility
- promise compensation
- invent disruption facts/statistics
- publish unverified competitor pricing
- alter FlightClaimly pricing/terms
- represent themselves as legal counsel
- launch material paid spend without approved budget
- send high-value publisher/authority outreach in FlightClaimly's name without approval

## Hiring decision rule

We are not hiring the cheapest applicant.

Hire the candidate who best combines:
**execution speed + taste + distribution thinking + factual discipline + clear communication**.

A USD 10–12/hour operator who produces useful work with little supervision is preferable to a USD 5–7/hour generalist who creates review overhead.
